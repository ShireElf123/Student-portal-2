How We Test AI Website Builders

Every score on this site comes from the same repeatable process. Here it is, in full — so you can judge our judgments.

By BuildAI Reviews Editorial Team · Last updated October 9, 2026

1. We pay for our own access

We subscribe to every tool we review using our own money, or use its free tier where one exists. Vendors never receive review drafts, never approve content, and never know our publication dates. Affiliate relationships (see our disclosure) are managed separately from testing and never influence scores.

2. We build real projects

We don't score tools from marketing pages or 10-minute demos. For each builder we create realistic projects — business sites, portfolios, blogs, small stores — and take them through the entire lifecycle: initial AI generation, editing, publishing to a real domain where possible, and monitoring load performance over days.

3. The scoring rubric

CategoryWeightWhat we measure
Ease of use20%Time from signup to published site; decisions forced on the user; help resources
AI capability25%Quality of generated copy/layouts/images; how much editing output actually needs
Design quality20%Default output polish; typography and spacing; animation and interaction options
Features & ecosystem15%Business tools, e-commerce, CMS, integrations, portability
Value for money10%Price vs capability at each tier; free-plan usefulness; renewal fairness
Performance & SEO10%Page speed on real published sites; meta/structured-data controls; indexing basics

Scores are weighted averages, rounded to one decimal. A score above 9.0 means we recommend the tool without reservation for its target audience; 8.0–8.9 means recommend with specific caveats; below 8.0 means recommend only for particular use cases.

4. We re-verify continuously

AI builders ship fast. We re-check pricing, plans, and headline claims at least quarterly, and every page shows when it was last verified. If a tool changes materially, we re-test and update the review — with the change noted on the page.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't accept payment for placement or scores.
  • We don't publish reviews of tools we haven't used hands-on.
  • We don't hide negatives to protect affiliate income — every review, including our #1 pick, lists real cons.
  • We don't use vendor-supplied screenshots as substitutes for our own testing notes.

6. Corrections policy

If you spot a factual error — a wrong price, an outdated feature claim — email hello@buildaireviews.com. We verify and correct quickly, and note the correction on the page.
