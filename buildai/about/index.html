We Build the Sites Before We Review the Builders

BuildAI Reviews exists because most "reviews" of AI tools are rewritten press releases. We do it differently.

Our story

We started in 2024 as a small team of web builders — people who ship real sites for real businesses — who kept hitting the same problem: every AI website builder promised "a professional site in seconds," and almost none of the write-ups online had actually tried it. So we started testing them ourselves, with the same projects, the same criteria, and our own money.

Since then we've put 12 AI builders through that process, spent 60+ hours per major review cycle building real sites, and published only what we could defend. When a tool underperforms, we say so — including in reviews of tools we earn commissions on.

How we stay independent

  • We self-fund our testing. Subscriptions are paid out of our own pocket. No vendor sponsors our reviews.
  • Affiliate income never touches scores. Commissions flow through our disclosed affiliate links, but our testing rubric is fixed and public — see the methodology.
  • We show our work. Reviews describe what we actually built and observed, including negatives.
  • We stay current. Pricing and feature claims are re-verified quarterly and dated on every page.

Who this site is for

Small business owners, solopreneurs, freelancers, and agencies trying to choose an AI website builder without wasting money on the wrong one. If that's you, start with our 2026 rankings, read the head-to-head comparisons, and check the methodology if you want to stress-test our thinking.
