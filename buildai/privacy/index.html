Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What we collect

Email addresses — only when you voluntarily enter them in one of our newsletter/guide forms. We use them to send you what you asked for and, occasionally, genuinely useful related content. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing works immediately.

Form and click data — when you submit a form or click an outbound /go/ link, we log basic details (which link, which page, a timestamp) so we can understand which tools readers care about. This data is not used to build advertising profiles.

Standard server logs — like all websites, our hosting keeps routine logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and reliability.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • We never share your email with third parties except the service that delivers our emails, under a data-processing agreement.
  • We never set third-party advertising cookies.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click an affiliate link (our /go/ links), the destination vendor may set its own cookie to attribute a purchase to us. That cookie is governed by the vendor's own privacy policy. Our /go/ redirects themselves do not set cookies.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time — email hello@buildaireviews.com and we'll action it promptly. These rights apply regardless of where you live (including under GDPR, POPIA, and CCPA-style frameworks).

Data retention

We keep email addresses until you unsubscribe, and anonymise analytics/click data after 24 months.

Children

This site is intended for adults making purchasing decisions. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it at the top of this page with a new date.

Contact: hello@buildaireviews.com
