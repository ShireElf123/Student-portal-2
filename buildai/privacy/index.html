Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What we collect

Form submissions — if Netlify Forms is enabled for the deployed site, information you submit through a form may be processed by the site host so the publisher can respond to your request. The site’s form handling and retention settings must be verified after deployment. Do not submit passwords, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Outbound-link click data — the current site script can record the provider/link, page, link label, timestamp and referring page. It stores a copy in your browser’s local storage and attempts to submit click details to the site through a Netlify form. Optional analytics events are sent only if an analytics function is present on the page. The live configuration, access controls and retention period must be verified before making stronger privacy promises.

Hosting logs — the hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information and requested paths for security and service operation, according to its current policies and configuration.

How information is handled

  • We do not intend to sell or rent personal information. This site’s actual data flows and hosting configuration should be reviewed periodically.
  • Information submitted through forms may be processed by the hosting provider and used to respond to the request.
  • The site’s click-tracking script uses browser local storage. Provider websites may use their own cookies or tracking when you follow an outbound link; their policies apply on their sites.

Affiliate links and cookies

Current /go/ links redirect to provider websites without verified affiliate tracking parameters. A provider may apply its own cookies or tracking after you leave this site. Review that provider’s privacy policy before continuing.

Your rights

You may ask about personal data held by the site or request correction or deletion. Use the private privacy-request form below once the site is deployed and its form handling has been verified. Do not post personal information in public GitHub issues. Applicable rights depend on the law and circumstances; this notice is not a claim that every jurisdiction grants identical rights.

Data retention

Retention depends on the form and hosting configuration. The site’s exact retention schedule has not been independently verified; it should be documented and applied consistently before collecting information at scale.

Children

This site is intended for adults making purchasing decisions. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

Privacy request

Use this form for privacy-related questions or requests. Do not include passwords, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information. Form submission will work only after the site is deployed and Netlify Forms is confirmed to be enabled.
