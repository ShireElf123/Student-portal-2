Affiliate Disclosure

Plain English, no legalese: how we make money and how we keep our opinions clean.

The short version

Current link status: The links in this version route directly to providers’ official websites. Affiliate tracking and commission arrangements have not been verified, so do not assume that clicking a link earns the publisher a commission. If genuine affiliate links are introduced later, they will be clearly identified and this disclosure will be updated before publication.

What that means in practice

  • Current /go/ links redirect to providers' official websites without verified affiliate tracking parameters. We will identify affiliate links and update this disclosure if a real affiliate relationship is confirmed.
  • If verified affiliate links are introduced, they will be clearly identified on the relevant pages as well as explained here.
  • Do not assume that a specific vendor pays us unless the current link or disclosure confirms it. Affiliate participation and link tracking must be verified before publication; this page should be updated when those relationships change.

Editorial independence

  • They don't set scores. Our scoring rubric is fixed and public. Testing is finished before any commercial consideration.
  • They should not decide recommendations. Any future affiliate relationship must be disclosed, and recommendations should be based on the stated evidence and reader needs.
  • They don't write copy. Vendors never review, edit, or approve our content.

Why you can trust this model

Affiliate links can create a financial incentive, so readers should consider the disclosure alongside the review’s evidence and limitations. A commission does not prove a product is suitable, and no ranking or score should be justified by the commission rate. Any statement about the publisher’s current earnings, paid testing or vendor relationships must be accurate and verifiable.

Legal note

This disclosure is made in accordance with the FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255) and equivalent consumer-protection rules in other jurisdictions, including South Africa's Consumer Protection Act.
