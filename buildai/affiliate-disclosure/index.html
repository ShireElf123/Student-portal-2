Affiliate Disclosure

Plain English, no legalese: how we make money and how we keep our opinions clean.

The short version

Some links on BuildAI Reviews are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. Prices are identical whether you use our link or go directly.

What that means in practice

  • Affiliate links on this site are clearly marked and routed through our /go/ links (for example buildaireviews.com/go/durable), which redirect to the vendor's official site with our tracking parameter.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships at the top of every page that contains affiliate links — not just here.
  • We currently participate in affiliate programs for tools we review, including programs operated by Durable, Dorik, Framer, Wix, and 10Web (directly or via their affiliate networks). This list updates as programs change.

What commissions never do

  • They don't set scores. Our scoring rubric is fixed and public. Testing is finished before any commercial consideration.
  • They don't decide rankings. Our #1 pick has real cons listed, and some tools that pay higher commissions rank lower on our site.
  • They don't write copy. Vendors never review, edit, or approve our content.

Why you can trust this model

Affiliate income keeps this site free to read and funds our self-paid testing subscriptions. Because commissions only happen when you're happy enough to sign up, our incentives align with giving you an honest recommendation: a bad recommendation costs us more in lost trust than any commission pays.

Legal note

This disclosure is made in accordance with the FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255) and equivalent consumer-protection rules in other jurisdictions, including South Africa's Consumer Protection Act.
