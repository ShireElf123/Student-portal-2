What We Found Testing Both

Dorik got us to "client-presentable" fastest: its AI generated sections we were happy to keep, and AI Quick Style let us explore visual directions in one click. Framer's pages took longer, but the final results had more motion, more personality, and more precise responsive behaviour — and we could export the code when the project ended.

The deciding question isn't capability; it's appetite. How many hours do you want to spend building? Dorik compresses that number dramatically with AI. Framer makes every hour count toward a more distinctive result.

Choose Dorik if…

  • You want AI to do heavy lifting on copy, sections and images
  • You're building client sites with white-label delivery
  • You need built-in e-commerce or memberships
  • You want great design with a shallower learning curve

Choose Framer if…

  • You want best-in-class animations and interactions
  • You have design/dev skills and enjoy the craft
  • You want to try everything free before paying
  • Code export or portability matters to you
