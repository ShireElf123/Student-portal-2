Durable AI vs Dorik AI — Speed vs Design Control

We built the same type of business site with both. One got us live in minutes; the other got us a more beautiful result. Here's the honest trade-off.

Quick answer

Consider Durable if you value a guided starting point and a small-business workflow. Consider Dorik if visual editing and design control are higher priorities. What We Found Testing Both

Durable is designed to reduce the work needed to get an initial business site started. Dorik offers more hands-on visual control. I have not run a timed, comparable build of both products, so I cannot substantiate generation-time comparisons or claim that one consistently needs fewer edits. Try the same brief in both tools before committing if output quality is decisive.

The business tooling tilts further toward Durable: its CRM captured our test enquiries and invoicing worked out of the box on the Business plan. Dorik counters with agency superpowers — white-label delivery, CMS collections, and membership support — plus multilingual AI copy that Durable can't match.

Choose Durable if…

  • You want a site live in under an hour
  • You're a non-designer who needs a professional result
  • You want CRM and invoicing baked in
  • Budget matters ($12/mo vs $18/mo)

Choose Dorik if…

  • Design quality matters more than setup speed
  • You're building client sites or white-label work
  • You need e-commerce functionality
  • You want multilingual AI content
