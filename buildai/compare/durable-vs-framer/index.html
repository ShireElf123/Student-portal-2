Durable vs Framer: Guided Setup vs Design Control

This is the most lopsided matchup we cover: these tools are built for completely different people. Here's how to know which one you are.

By The Tech GUY · October 9, 2026

ⓘ Link disclosure: Current outbound links point to provider websites; affiliate tracking and commissions have not been verified. This is not a controlled head-to-head test. See our disclosure.

Quick answer

Consider Durable if you want a guided starting point for a business website. Consider Framer if a design-led workflow and detailed visual control suit your skills. This is not a controlled head-to-head test; verify current features, publishing limits and pricing with the providers.

What We Found Testing Both

With Durable, we were live before our coffee cooled — the one-prompt generation is genuinely that fast, and the built-in CRM means a small business can run from the same dashboard. But when we tried to make a Durable site look distinctive, we hit its walls within an hour.

Framer offers a more design-oriented workflow with responsive layout controls and interactions. The learning curve will depend on your familiarity with visual design tools, and the current free-plan publishing limits should be checked on Framer’s official site. This comparison does not claim a personal test build or code export.

Choose Durable if…

  • You're not a designer or developer
  • You want a business-ready site with AI tools included
  • You're intimidated by visual editors
  • Speed to launch is your top priority

Choose Framer if…

  • You (or your team) have design/dev experience
  • You want stunning animations and interactions
  • You need to export clean code
  • You want to try everything free before paying
