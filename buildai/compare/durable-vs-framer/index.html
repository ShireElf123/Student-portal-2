Durable AI vs Framer — Done-For-You Speed vs Total Control

This is the most lopsided matchup we cover: these tools are built for completely different people. Here's how to know which one you are.

Quick answer

Choose Durable AI if you're not a designer and want a business-ready site in minutes ($12/mo, with CRM and invoicing included). Choose Framer if you have design or development experience and want the most visually impressive result possible — it has the best free plan in the industry and pixel-perfect control.

What We Found Testing Both

With Durable, we were live before our coffee cooled — the one-prompt generation is genuinely that fast, and the built-in CRM means a small business can run from the same dashboard. But when we tried to make a Durable site look distinctive, we hit its walls within an hour.

Framer is the inverse experience. Our first page took real effort — understanding stacks, breakpoints, and the layout model — but the result had scroll animations and polish that no other builder here can touch, and we could export the code at the end. Its free plan also means you can try the entire experience before spending anything.

Choose Durable if…

  • You're not a designer or developer
  • You want a business-ready site with AI tools included
  • You're intimidated by visual editors
  • Speed to launch is your top priority

Choose Framer if…

  • You (or your team) have design/dev experience
  • You want stunning animations and interactions
  • You need to export clean code
  • You want to try everything free before paying
