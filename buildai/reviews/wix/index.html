What Is Wix AI?

Wix is the most established name in website building — over 250 million users worldwide — and its AI setup turns the old "answer a questionnaire" flow into a conversation. You chat with Wix's AI about your business, and it assembles a site with pages, copy, images, and relevant features (bookings, store, forms) based on what you describe. Then the full Wix editor opens up for refinement.

That second half is the real story: behind the AI front door sits the most app-rich platform in this comparison — 900+ apps, mature e-commerce, restaurant and booking systems, email marketing, and genuinely good SEO tools.

Our Testing Experience

We built a service business site (a tutoring studio) through the AI chat, then a small 12-product store. The AI conversation was smooth and produced a sensible starting structure — but the generated copy needed more rewriting than Durable's, and the initial design felt template-y until we invested time in the editor.

Where Wix won us back: capability. Adding booking slots, taking a test payment, and wiring up an email capture took minutes with built-in or first-party apps — features that Durable and Framer simply don't have natively. The SEO Setup Checklist walked us through meta tags, structured data, and sitemap submission in a way beginners can actually follow.

The costs: heavier pages than Framer or Dorik output (still within acceptable Core Web Vitals limits in our tests, but noticeably heavier), and a dashboard that can overwhelm newcomers with upsells. Wix's public Trustpilot rating sits mid-range (~3.5/5), mostly reflecting billing and support friction — worth knowing before subscribing.
