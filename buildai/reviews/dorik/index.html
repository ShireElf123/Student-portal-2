What Is Dorik AI?

Dorik is a no-code website builder that leans hard into design quality. Its AI features cover three jobs: generating complete page sections from a prompt, writing and rewriting copy in any language, and producing images from text. Unlike builders that generate one opinionated site and call it done, Dorik treats AI as a co-pilot inside a full visual editor — you stay in control of layout, spacing, and structure at every step.

It also ships features professionals care about: CMS collections for blogs and listings, membership-site capability, and white-label options that let agencies deliver sites without Dorik branding. Third-party reviewers agree with our take — Dorik holds a 4.4/5 on G2 and 4.5/5 on Capterra as of mid-2026.

Our Testing Experience

We built two projects with Dorik: a five-page marketing site for a fictional consultancy, and a small blog-driven site using CMS collections. The AI-generated sections needed noticeably less cleanup than most competitors — typography and spacing choices were consistently tasteful, which matters when you're shipping client work.

Two details stood out. First, the AI Quick Style feature: one click re-themes a whole page's look, and we used it repeatedly to explore directions before committing. Second, the built-in AI image generator meant we never left the editor for placeholder visuals. Both saved real hours.

The trade-offs: Dorik's editor has more knobs than Durable or Wix, so a complete beginner will feel the learning curve (we estimate a day or two to feel fluent). And there's no free plan — you must pay before publishing on a custom domain, which is a real friction point for people who just want to experiment.
