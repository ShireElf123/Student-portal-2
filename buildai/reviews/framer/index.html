What Is Framer?

Framer started as a prototyping tool for product designers and grew into a full website publisher. Its editor is closer to Figma than to Wix: you compose with flexible layout primitives, define responsive behaviour per breakpoint, and add scroll-triggered animations that would need real JavaScript anywhere else. Published sites are fast, static, and served on a global CDN.

Its AI features focus on assistance: generating page layouts from a prompt, rewriting sections, and translating entire sites into other languages. It won't hand you a finished business site in 30 seconds the way Durable does — the AI gets you 60% there, and your taste does the rest.

Our Testing Experience

We built three pages: a SaaS landing page generated from a prompt, a portfolio assembled from sections, and a hand-built page with scroll animations. The AI-generated landing page was the best raw output we've seen from any "generate from prompt" flow — sensible hierarchy, real design opinion — though we still spent several hours polishing it to client-ready standard.

The animation system is where Framer earns its reputation. Effects that take a developer an afternoon elsewhere took us minutes with Framer's built-in transitions and scroll effects. On the other side of the ledger: our first hour was mostly spent understanding Framer's layout model (stacks, grids, breakpoints). Anyone coming from a drag-and-drop wizard will need patience.

One honest caveat from our research: Framer's public Trustpilot rating has trended low, driven largely by billing and support complaints rather than the product itself. Our own support experience was fine, but if you subscribe, use a payment method you can manage and keep an eye on renewal dates.
