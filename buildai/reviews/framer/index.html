What Is Framer?

Framer started as a prototyping tool for product designers and grew into a full website publisher. Its editor is closer to Figma than to Wix: you compose with flexible layout primitives, define responsive behaviour per breakpoint, and add scroll-triggered animations that would need real JavaScript anywhere else. Published sites are fast, static, and served on a global CDN.

Its AI features focus on assistance: generating page layouts from a prompt, rewriting sections, and translating entire sites into other languages. It won't hand you a finished business site in 30 seconds the way Durable does — the AI gets you 60% there, and your taste does the rest.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Best-in-class animations and interaction design
  • Pixel-perfect responsive control per breakpoint
  • Best free plan of any AI builder — publish a full site free
  • Clean, fast static output on a global CDN
  • AI layout generation is genuinely useful as a starting point
  • Built-in site translation for multilingual launches

Cons

  • Steepest learning curve in this comparison
  • AI is assistive, not done-for-you generation
  • No native e-commerce (relies on third-party embeds)
  • Mixed support reputation on public review platforms
  • Overkill for a simple one-page business site
