What Is Durable AI?

Durable is an AI-powered website builder aimed at small businesses and other users who want help getting an initial website draft started. Its current generation workflow, available features and publishing requirements should be checked on the official site. The review’s first-hand evidence is limited to a client project, not a timed test.

Unlike traditional website builders where you start from a blank template and drag things around, Durable starts you with something finished and lets you refine it. This reverses the usual friction of setup and is genuinely impressive in practice.

Beyond the website builder itself, Durable has evolved into a small-business platform — including a built-in CRM, invoicing, receipt tracking, and an AI assistant that can write blog posts or marketing copy on demand.

First-hand experience

I used Durable to build a website for a client. In my experience, the platform handled much of the initial website-building work, which helped move the project forward, and the client was happy with the result. This is a real project-based observation, not a controlled benchmark: I am not claiming a measured generation time or a repeatable result across several test sites.

I also observed the client website attracting traffic after it was published. Without analytics records and a controlled comparison, that observation does not establish that Durable caused the traffic or increased it. Traffic depends on many factors, including content, demand, promotion, links and indexing. I have not included a speed benchmark because I do not have a documented, comparable measurement to substantiate one here.

The practical question for a new project is how much control you need after the first draft. Before subscribing, check the current editor, plan limits, integrations and export or migration options against the site you need to maintain. This review does not claim to have benchmarked every feature or every plan.
