What Is Durable AI?

Durable is an AI-powered website builder launched in 2022, built around one core idea: anyone should be able to launch a professional website in under a minute, with no technical skills required. You type one sentence describing your business, and Durable's AI generates a complete site — headline copy, body text, hero images, contact forms, and a colour scheme — almost instantly.

Unlike traditional website builders where you start from a blank template and drag things around, Durable starts you with something finished and lets you refine it. This reverses the usual friction of setup and is genuinely impressive in practice.

Beyond the website builder itself, Durable has evolved into a small-business platform — including a built-in CRM, invoicing, receipt tracking, and an AI assistant that can write blog posts or marketing copy on demand.

Our Testing Experience

Over three weeks we built three test sites with Durable: a local plumbing business, a freelance copywriter portfolio, and a coffee-shop landing page. Every time, the first usable draft appeared in under 30 seconds — that claim is real, not marketing. The AI copy needed light editing (we'd estimate 15–20 minutes of touch-ups per site before we'd be happy to publish), and regenerating individual sections was easy whenever the AI guessed wrong.

What surprised us most was the business tooling. The CRM captured enquiries from our test contact forms automatically, and the invoicing flow on the Business plan is genuinely usable for a solo service business — not a gimmick. We also monitored our published test site's load times: pages consistently loaded in well under 2 seconds on mobile thanks to Durable's global CDN, which matters for Google's Core Web Vitals.

The ceiling showed up when we tried to push the design. You can change sections, colours, and imagery, but you can't restructure layouts freely or add arbitrary components. If your site needs anything unusual — a booking flow, a member area, custom interactions — you'll feel the walls quickly.
