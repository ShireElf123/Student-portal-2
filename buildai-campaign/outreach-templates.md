# Outreach Templates — The Tech GUY / BuildAI Reviews

Copy-paste-ready templates. Rules for all of them:
- **Answer first, brand second.** Journalists delete anything that reads as a pitch.
- **Nothing you can't prove.** No invented clients, numbers, or titles.
- **No public email address.** Where contact is needed, point to your contact form
  or the business channel you actually use.
- Personalise the bracketed `[...]` parts. An unpersonalised template is worse than none.
- Follow up **once**, after 5 business days. Then stop.

---

## 1. Journalist platform response (Qwoted / SourceBottle / Featured)

Use when a reporter asks for an expert on websites, AI tools, small business tech,
or digital marketing. Respond within a few hours — speed wins these.

> **[Answer the exact question in 2–3 direct sentences first.]**
>
> Example opening for an "AI website builders" query:
> "In my experience building client websites, AI builders have crossed the point
> where they produce a genuinely usable first draft in minutes — but the real work
> has shifted from building to editing. The platforms that save business owners the
> most time are the ones that handle structure, copy and basic SEO together,
> because those are the three things non-technical owners struggle with most."
>
> **[Then one concrete detail from real experience — a trade-off, a number you can
> defend, or a specific observation. Example:]**
> "I used one of these platforms to build a live site for a client: it handled the
> initial heavy lifting well and the client was happy with the result. The caveat I
> always flag is customisation — the more a business needs to deviate from the
> template, the more the time savings shrink."
>
> **Bio line (keep identical everywhere so Google connects the dots):**
> [Your name], founder of The Tech GUY — a technology and digital-services
> business covering website development, design, digital marketing and IT support.
> Publishes hands-on AI website builder reviews at BuildAI Reviews.
> [site URL]

**Length:** under 150 words total. **Tone:** plain, quotable, zero promotion.

## 3. Directory submission (Futurepedia / There's An AI For That / Product Hunt / local SA directories)

Most directories ask the same five things. Keep these identical everywhere:

- **Name:** BuildAI Reviews
- **One-liner:** Hands-on reviews and comparisons of AI website builders,
  published by a working web developer and digital-services business.
- **Description (50–80 words):** BuildAI Reviews compares AI website builders —
  Durable, Dorik, Framer, Wix AI and 10Web — for small businesses and freelancers.
  Each page labels its evidence: first-hand client-project experience, hands-on
  testing, or research-based assessment. Published by The Tech GUY, a technology
  and digital-services business.
- **Category:** AI website builders / Web development / Reviews
- **URL:** your production domain
