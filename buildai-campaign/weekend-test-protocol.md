# Weekend Test Protocol — BuildAI Reviews Original Research

**Goal:** one honest weekend of real testing that upgrades 4 research-based
reviews into hands-on evidence AND produces the site's main link magnet
(the data study). Budget: **8–10 hours** across Saturday/Sunday.

---

## The honesty rules (read first — these protect the whole site)

1. Record **only what actually happened**. If a tool crashes, times out, or
   surprises you — that's gold, not a problem. Real observations are the asset.
2. Use **the same brief for every builder** so comparisons are fair.
3. Time everything with one stopwatch/phone. Note start/stop per stage.
4. Screenshots are taken **as you go**, not reconstructed afterwards.
5. If you only finish 3 of 5 tools, publish 3. Partial-and-true beats
   complete-and-rushed. Say which tools weren't tested.
6. Free tiers / trials only. Do **not pay for subscriptions you can't afford**
   just for the test — note "tested on free tier; paid features not verified".
   That's honest and completely acceptable.

---

## The standard brief (identical for all 5 builders)

> A one-page website for **"Kasi Fresh", a fictional food-delivery kitchen in
> Soweto, Johannesburg**: hero with business name + tagline, services/menu
> section, opening hours, WhatsApp/order button, contact section, Google Maps
> placeholder. Mobile-first, clean, appetising.

Using a local, realistic brief makes the study genuinely useful to your target
reader (SA small business) and differentiates it from every US-based roundup.
Tell the AI builder exactly this prompt (paste the same text everywhere):

> "Create a website for Kasi Fresh, a food delivery kitchen in Soweto,
> Johannesburg. We cook and deliver township-favourite meals. Include a menu
> section, opening hours, a WhatsApp order button, and a contact section."

Save the exact prompt in `evidence/00-prompt.md`.

## Evidence folder structure (create before you start)

```
evidence/
├── 00-prompt.md                    ← the exact brief text, dated
├── timing-log.md                   ← running log, one line per stage
├── durable-01-first-draft.png
├── durable-02-edited.png
├── ...
└── notes.md                        ← anything unexpected, frustrations, wins
```

This folder is the difference between "we tested it" (a claim) and
"here are the screenshots and timings" (evidence). It also feeds the
E-E-A-T upgrade of each review page.

---

## What the data-study post gets (Month 3, page #7 of the calendar)

From your completed scorecards:

1. **Headline table:** tool × time-to-first-draft × edits-needed × publish cost × PageSpeed
2. **Per-tool mini sections:** best moment, worst moment, who it suits
3. **The honest verdicts:** which one you'd recommend for which business —
   including "none, if you need X"
4. **Evidence link/block:** "screenshots and timing log available on request"
   (or publish selected screenshots inline)
5. Pitch it: this table IS the journalist pitch ("we timed 5 AI builders on the
   same real South African business brief — data + screenshots attached")

## After the weekend

- Update each tested review: change its label from *Research-based assessment*
  to *Hands-on tested [date]*, weave in the specific observations, link the study
- Update the homepage + comparisons with the new evidence labels
- Social: 3 posts per tool ("Here's what happened when I made Durable build a
  Soweto food-delivery site") — real screenshots make these perform
- Journalist platforms: add "original research published" to your bio lines

**Total outputs from one weekend:** 4 upgraded reviews + 1 link-magnet study +
12+ social posts + a credibility anchor for every future pitch.
