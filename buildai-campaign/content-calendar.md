# 6-Month Content Calendar — BuildAI Reviews

Cadence: **2–3 pages per month.** Quality and evidence over volume — Google's
reviews system actively demotes thin affiliate pages, and one strong page beats
five rushed ones. Every entry lists the **evidence required** before publishing;
if the evidence doesn't exist yet, the page waits.

Publish rhythm that works: one page in week 1, one in week 3, social posts for
each on publish day + 3 days later.

## Every page ships with (non-negotiable checklist)

- [ ] Evidence label visible: hands-on / project experience / research-based
- [ ] FAQPage + appropriate Article/Review JSON-LD, matching visible content
- [ ] Internal links: pillar ↔ review ↔ comparison ↔ new page (2–4 links each way)
- [ ] Dated pricing/feature claims ("checked [date] on vendor's official page")
- [ ] Author byline: The Tech GUY, linked to /about/
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure above the first commercial link (already in template)
- [ ] Quick-answer box in the first 60 words (AI Overviews pull from these)
- [ ] 3 social posts drafted before publishing (see routine below)

## Weekly routine (the 45-minutes-a-day that compounds)

- **Daily (15 min):** respond to journalist queries on Qwoted/SourceBottle/Featured
- **Tue/Thu (45 min):** write/progress the month's pages
- **Friday (30 min):** 2 community answers (Reddit r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, Facebook SMB groups — genuinely help; your profile carries the site link, never spam links in answers)
- **Monthly:** one unlinked-mention sweep + one pricing re-verification pass
